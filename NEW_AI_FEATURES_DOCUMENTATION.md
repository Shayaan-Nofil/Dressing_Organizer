## 🤖 **Smart Wardrobe Assistant**

### **What It Does**
The Smart Wardrobe Assistant uses advanced AI to analyze your entire wardrobe and provide strategic fashion recommendations.

### **Key Features**

#### 1. **Wardrobe Gap Analysis**
- Identifies missing essential items in your wardrobe
- Finds underdeveloped clothing categories
- Suggests color palette improvements
- Provides an overall wardrobe score (1-10)

#### 2. **Strategic Purchase Recommendations**
- Suggests items that maximize outfit combinations
- Prioritizes purchases by importance (high/medium/low)
- Considers your budget and lifestyle
- Shows versatility scores for each recommended item
- Estimates cost ranges for purchases

#### 3. **Redundancy Detection**
- Identifies similar items you might want to donate
- Helps streamline your wardrobe
- Suggests which items to keep vs. replace

#### 4. **Smart Shopping List Generation**
- Creates budget-conscious shopping lists
- Shows potential new outfit combinations
- Breaks down budget by category (essentials, versatile pieces, trends)
- Considers seasonal needs

### **How to Use**
1. Navigate to **AI Assistant** in the main menu
2. Set your preferences (budget level, lifestyle, shopping budget)
3. Click **"Analyze My Wardrobe"** for comprehensive analysis
4. Click **"Generate Shopping List"** for purchase recommendations
5. Review detailed insights in expandable sections

## 🌍 **Social Context Advisor**

### **What It Does**
The Social Context Advisor ensures you're always appropriately dressed for any social, cultural, or professional situation.

### **Key Features**

#### 1. **Context-Aware Outfit Suggestions**
- Analyzes event type, location, and cultural context
- Considers attendee profile and social dynamics
- Provides time-appropriate recommendations
- Suggests outfits from your existing wardrobe

#### 2. **Cultural Appropriateness Assessment**
- Checks outfits for cultural sensitivity
- Provides specific feedback on cultural considerations
- Suggests modifications for better cultural fit
- Educates about cultural customs and etiquette

#### 3. **Social Dynamics Analysis**
- Evaluates how your outfit fits the group setting
- Assesses attention level (appropriate/too flashy/understated)
- Provides confidence-boosting insights
- Considers practical factors (comfort, mobility, weather)

#### 4. **Alternative Options**
- Offers multiple outfit choices for different comfort levels
- Provides more conservative or bold alternatives
- Explains when to choose each option

#### 5. **Manual Outfit Checker**
- Select any items from your wardrobe
- Get instant cultural appropriateness feedback
- Learn about potential issues before wearing

### **How to Use**
1. Navigate to **Social Advisor** in the main menu
2. Fill out the event context form:
   - Event type (wedding, business meeting, dinner, etc.)
   - Location/Country
   - Cultural context and customs
   - Attendee profile
   - Time of day and duration
   - Formality level
3. Click **"Get Social Context Advice"** for AI recommendations
4. Or use **"Check Your Own Outfit"** to verify a specific combination
